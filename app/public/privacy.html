We do not ask for your phone number or location, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. Signing in is optional: you can play without it. The game does not use cookies. It stores your identity, name, settings and muted players in your browser's local storage.

Who can see it

Other players in your room see your display name, chat, reactions, nicknames and the things they throw at each other. Everyone, in any room, can see display names and results in the Hall of Shame. Hands of cards are private: the server sends each player only their own cards. We do not sell your data. We share it only if the law requires it.

Where it is stored

On the server we run at [hosting provider and country/region]. Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and if you open Sign in with Google your browser talks to Google, under Google's own privacy policy.

Your rights

Open My profile in the game and choose Download my data to get a copy, or Delete my data to remove your profile, stats, Hall of Shame entries, chat messages and uploaded songs from the server. Deleting also resets your browser identity. To correct data, or to complain, write to [grievance contact email]. If you are not satisfied you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

The game is meant for people aged 18 and over. If you are younger, use it only with a parent or guardian's consent. [Operator to confirm the age policy.]
