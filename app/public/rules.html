How to play

Short version: get rid of your cards first. The last player holding cards is the Donkey.

The goal

Donkey of the Day is a card game for four or five players with one 52-card deck. There are no jokers and no trump suit. The Ace is high and the 2 is low.

You win a hand by emptying your hand. Whoever still has cards at the end is the Donkey for that hand and takes a loss. Collect enough losses and you become the Donkey of the Day.

Setup

  1. Everyone draws one card. The player with the highest card arranges the seating order around the table.
  2. A second draw decides the first dealer: the lowest card deals. Ties are redrawn.

Dealing

The dealer deals clockwise, handing out batches of their choice, from a single card up to a player’s whole share at once. Everyone ends up with the same number of cards.

Five players? 52 does not split evenly, so 2 extra cards go to one player. The host can choose to give one extra card each to two players instead. The extra-card seat moves one place clockwise every hand.

Tricks

  • The Ace of Spades opens the first trick of the match. Whoever holds it leads.
  • Each player in turn plays one card. You must follow the suit that was led if you have it.
  • If everyone follows suit, the trick is simply discarded. The player with the highest card of the led suit leads the next trick, and may lead any card.

Cutting

If you cannot follow the led suit, you cut. Choose one or more cards of a single other suit and play them. Then:

  • The player who played the highest card of the led suit picks up the table pile and your cut cards.
  • Everyone sees the cards you gave for 20 seconds.
  • You lead the next trick.

Tip: giving away a lot of cards in a cut is how you get rid of a bad hand. Sending a big pile to a player who is already holding many cards is even better.

Going out

When you play your last card you are out and safe for this hand. Emptying your hand only counts once the trick or pickup finishes, so your last card can still collect a pickup.

  • If the player who should lead is already out, the lead passes clockwise.
  • If everyone empties on the final trick, the highest card of that trick takes the loss.
  • Stalemate: if the same position comes round three times in one hand, whoever holds the most cards takes the loss. On a tie, the player due to lead takes it.

The Donkey and the series

The last player holding cards is the Donkey for the hand. They take one loss and deal next.

Before the match the host chooses a series: first to 2, 3 or 5 losses. Losses do not need to be consecutive. The first player to reach the target becomes Donkey of the Day, dated in the room’s timezone, and goes on the Hall of Shame.

Fun rules

These are options the host turns on or off in the lobby. They never change the basic rules above.

Shuffle power

After a hand, the Donkey picks how the next deck is shuffled. The shuffle also decides how the cards are dealt: how many cards each player gets per pass. Then another player gets to cut the deck, knock (no cut) or do a Double Kaat. If time runs out, the machine shuffles.

Magic cards

With Magic cards on, the first player out in a hand can earn a magic card (you can hold two). Use it while you are out.

👁️ PeekLook at one random card from another player’s hand.
🛡️ ShieldBlocks one pickup coming your way this hand.
🔁 SwapOn your turn, trade one random card with the next player still in.
↩️ ReverseThe next trick is played in the opposite direction.

Bets on the Donkey

Once you are out, you can bet on who will be the Donkey. A correct bet earns you a point in the match standings.

Mute the Donkey

When on, the player who was the Donkey cannot chat during the next hand. Their moves still count, of course.

Banter level

The commentary and tease lines can be set to off, halka (mild) or teekha (spicy). The default follows your device setting. A mild word filter is always on in chat.

Timers and dropped connections

  • Each move has 90 seconds. When time runs out, your lowest legal card is played, or one lowest card is cut.
  • After two automatic moves in a row, the game pauses for up to 2 minutes for you. If you do not return, the match ends without an award.
  • If your phone drops, your seat is held. Moves are made for you if it is your turn. Open the game again, or sign in on another device and use My games to resume.
  • A cut is allowed on the opening Spades trick too.

FAQ

Can I cut with cards of two different suits?

No. A cut is one or more cards of a single suit, and it cannot be the led suit.

What if I can follow suit but would rather cut?

You can’t. If you have the led suit you must play it.

Who leads after a cut?

The player who cut. The pile and their cards go to the highest led-suit card.

I left. Can I come back?

Yes. Your seat is held. Join with the same room code on the same device, or sign in to resume from another.

Do bots play by the same rules?

Yes. A bot can be the Donkey too. The host can set each bot’s skill level in the lobby.
