Quick answers

How many people can play?

Four or five. If you are short, the host can fill empty seats with bots. Anyone else can join as a spectator.

Do I need an account?

No. Your browser is enough. An optional Google sign-in adds match history, resume on any device and a list of signed-in devices.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open the page in Safari and use Share, then Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app.

Is there music?

Yes, and it is optional. The room plays one song for everyone, or you can use your own music app instead.

Can I play with strangers?

You can open a public room that others can find, or keep it private and share the code only with friends.
